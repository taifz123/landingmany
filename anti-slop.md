# Design Direction

Use a clear editorial hierarchy, thoughtful asymmetry and generous whitespace. Avoid repetitive equal card grids, generic stock imagery, excessive gradients, animation for its own sake, unsupported testimonials, invented project results and placeholder buttons. Use white and warm-neutral surfaces with restrained teal and gold accents.

Keep copy concise, specific to website design and development, and useful to a prospective client. Preserve the business phone number and offer straightforward call and text actions. Label illustrative concepts honestly and do not add personal identifying information.
