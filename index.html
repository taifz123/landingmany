02 / The services

What goes into
your website.

Some businesses need one good page. Others need room for a catalogue of work. We plan the pages around the questions your customers ask.

01Page planning

We map out the services, projects and contact details people need to find. That gives us a page list to price before design starts.

02Custom visual design

Your type, colour, imagery and page layouts are designed together. You review the proposed direction before we build the site.

03Responsive development

We build business websites and landing pages with layouts for phones, tablets and larger screens. Photos are sized for the space they occupy.

04Enquiries and launch checks

We place your preferred contact options where customers need them, then check links, navigation and page loading before launch.

03 / The process

From first call to launch.

  1. 01 / Discuss

    Set the brief.

    Tell us about your business and your current site, if you have one. We’ll agree on the pages, timing and price in writing.

  2. 02 / Design

    See the direction.

    Review the layout with your own services and imagery in place. This is when we adjust what doesn’t feel right.

  3. 03 / Develop

    Go live.

    We build the approved design and check it on small and large screens. You see the finished site before it’s published.
