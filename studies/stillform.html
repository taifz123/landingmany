The first impression

Here, the surface of a pot matters as much as its shape. A large, closely cropped still life sits beside a short material description, with an object index for visitors who want to browse.

On a smaller screen

On a narrow screen, the material note moves above the photograph. The two no longer compete for width, and the glaze and clay remain visible.
