Sunday Table.

A café site built around the next visit.
Independent concept, not client work.

Choose a Sunday Table website preview

Design notes

The thinking
behind it.

The first impression

A first-time visitor usually has two questions: what’s on the menu, and where is the café? Both sit in the top navigation. An olive masthead and a sunlit counter give a sense of the room before the visit.

On a smaller screen

On a phone, the masthead fits above a portrait crop of the café. The location link sits below the photo, away from the lettering and the coffee badge.
